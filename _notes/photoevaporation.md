photoevaporation is caused by Extreme-UV or X-ray radiation from the star (or from nearby stars in some cases). the radiation heats up the surface of the disk and gives escape velocity.

Typically causes 10^-8 to 10^-10 M_sun/year. Can kick in very quickly after accretion weakens, and the dispersal happens ~10^5 years scale.

Can be observed by forbidden lines and blueshifted/redshifted line profile.
