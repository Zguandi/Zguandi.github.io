# Mars

| mass              | 0.10 M_Earth                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Distance to sun   | 1.5 AU                                                                   |
| Atmosphere        | 0.01 atm                                                                 |
| Magnetic Field    | None                                                                     |
| Composition       | No surface water, possible water pockets under surface, Methane presence |
| tectonic activity | tiny activity                                                            |
| Habitability      | Quite habitable                                                          |

Mars lost its magnetic field ~ 4Gyrs ago. (as outer core cooled and [[magnetic dynamo]] stopped)

Ice caps and undersurface water, and traces of water on surface.

Solar radiation breaks methane in 300 years, so presence of methane suggest sources such as possible life forms.
