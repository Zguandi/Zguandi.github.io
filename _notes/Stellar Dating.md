To date the time of formation, we can use [[Stellar Metallicity]] and other sources of informations.

In terms of Age:
- In [[Stellar Clusters]], and stellar groups, we can use *turn-off* points in M_star.
- High mass stars (O,B stars): indicates age less than 100Myr.
- Stellar Rotation of F,G,U
	- Faster Rotating  means more active and generally <1Gyr age
	- Slower means older
- Observation of [[Planetary Nebula (PN)]]
