Consider a Spherical mass that is collapsing and has a Luminocity of $L$

The Radiation pressure acting on the boundary $F_{rad}=\sigma_T P_{rad}n_e=\frac{\sigma_T n_e L}{4\pi r^2 c}$,

At critical mass/luminosity, the Radiation pressure is in equillibrium with the $F_{grav}=\frac{GM\rho(r)}{r^2}\approx \frac{GM m_p n_e}{r^2}$

This solves to give $L_{edd}=\frac{4\pi G c m_p}{\sigma_T}M_{edd}$.

beyond this Luminosity, the accretion should not happen.
