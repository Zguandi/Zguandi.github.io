Subhalo mass function ($M$ mass of host halo, $m$ mass of subhalo):
$$
\frac{dM}{d \ln(m/M)}\approx \frac{f_0}{\beta\Gamma(1-\gamma)}(\frac{m}{\beta M})^{-\gamma}\exp \left[-\frac{m}{\beta M} \right]
$$
A *good fit* is achieved for most simulations and $\gamma \sim 0.9,0.1<\beta<0.5$.

The $f_0 = \frac{1}{M}\int m \frac{dM}{dm}dm$ is the mean subhalo mass fraction.

Subhalos have survival time depending on its [[Merger Tree]].
