Star and disks form with the same material and element abundance is reasonably similar.

As disk evolves, disks cool down and elements condensate into solids.

Also the closer to the star, the hotter it gets, and there is a radial condensation sequence.

This accounts for the reason why inner planets are more metallic and outer planets are more gaseous and has more volatiles.

Also there is temeprature restriction for sillicate condensation: 1500K
Also there is upper limit of solid core with carbon: 2000K (This is considered the start of [[Planet Formation Main]])
