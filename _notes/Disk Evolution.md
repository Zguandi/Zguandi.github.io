[[Protoplanetary Disks]] is evolving in time:
- mass [[Accretion rate]] onto forming star and planets
- Stellar Photoevaporation, stellar winds (also caused by nearby stars)
- Viscous Evolution, see [[Viscosity]].
- Magnetic effects
- Structural formation 
	-  [[Self-gravity]] clumping
	- planet open gaps and spiral wakes evolve
	- Instabilities
- Temperature evolution

# Typical timescale

Sun-like 3-5 Myrs
Large stars have <1Myr
