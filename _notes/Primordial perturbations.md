At the end of the [[Inflation theory]] era. Perturbations are generated from quantum fluctuations.
- **Microscopic Scales** gest blown up to **Macroscopic Scales**.

The inflaton field Lagrangian density:
$$
\mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{2}\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi-V(\phi)
$$
In the inflation era, metric:
$$
g(t) = -dt^2+e^{Ht}(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)
$$

The primordial fluctuations that exists are:

| Mode                           | d.o.f                                   |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| $h_{ij}^{TT}$                  | 2 ([[Primordial gravitational waves]])  |
| $\delta \phi$, $\Phi$, $\Psi$. | 1 ([[Primordial scalar perturbations]]) |
