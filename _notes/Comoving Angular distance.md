Recall in Eucledian Space:

For small angles: $D\sim r\Delta \theta$, for $D$ the angular diameter of a distant object.

Now in context of cosmology, consider an object with transverse Physical size of $L_{phys}$.

$$
L_{phys}^2=-ds^2=-c^2dt^2+a(t)^2(d\chi^2+r^2(\chi)(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta d\phi^2))
$$
This gives:
$$
L_{phys}=ar(\chi)d\phi
$$
This defines the **Comoving Angular distance** $r(\chi)$ and the **Physical Angular Distance** $d_A$:
$$
d_A = \frac{L_{phys}}{\Delta\phi}=\frac{aL_{comoving}}{\Delta\phi}=ar(\chi)
$$
For [[FLRW metric]] without spatial curvature:
$$
r(\chi)=\chi;d_A = a\chi
$$
With $\chi$ the [[Comoving distance]].
