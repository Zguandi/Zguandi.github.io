The DM halos after [[Virialisation]] usualt take an ellipsoidal shape, with three major axies $a_1>a_2>a_3$ and three axies derections $\vec{g}_1,\vec{g}_2,\vec{g}_3$.

The halo *flattening parameter* $S = \frac{a_3}{a_1}$ describes how flat a halo is.

The Halo rotation inertia is:
$$
I_{ij}=\frac{1}{5}M\begin{pmatrix}
b^2+c^2&0&0\\0&a^2+c^2&0\\0&0&a^2+b^2
\end{pmatrix}
$$
Also a *triaxiality parameter* $T = \frac{a^2-b^2}{a^2-c^2}$ is sometimes used to describe whether this halo is prolate $T\sim 1$ or oblate $T\sim 0$.
