Color index is usually taken as the subtraction of different filter bands, and reflects the stellar temperature.

e.g. B-V (Blue [[Magnitudes]] - Visual [[Magnitudes]])
$$
Color = 
\begin{cases}
Redder & B-V>0\\
Bluer & B-V<0
\end{cases}
$$
