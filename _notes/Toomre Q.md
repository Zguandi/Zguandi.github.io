Toomre Q is defined as:
$$
Q=\frac{c_s \Omega}{\pi G \Sigma}
$$
Is the ratio between disk orbital motion and disk self gravity

A more detailed derivation is seen in [[Core collapse]] model discussions.

- When Q<1 disk is gravitationally unstable
- disk fragmentation happens at Q<1.4
- disk spiral arms form at Q<1.7
