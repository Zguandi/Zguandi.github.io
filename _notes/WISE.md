Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer. Originaly devised to observe Near-Earth-Objects (NEOs).

## Wiki page
[WISE introduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide-field_Infrared_Survey_Explorer).

## Wavelengths
- **Blue**: 3.4 microns
- **Green**: 4.6 microns
- **Red**: 12 microns

## Angular resolution

The WISE telescope has an angular resolution at around $6''$. in terms of spherical harmonics, this corresponds to a $\ell_{psf} = \pi/\delta_\theta \approx 108000$. This means that in usually sense, the correlation done at $\ell \lesssim 4000$ is not subject to the PSF effects.
