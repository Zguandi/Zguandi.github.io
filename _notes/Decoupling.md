Decoupling in cosmology refer to the decoupling between the [[Cosmic Microwave Background]] Photons (will be CMB photons) and the free electrons.

The reaction is Thompson Scattering:
$$
\gamma+e\rightarrow \gamma + e
$$
Similar to [[Recombination]], happens at $z_{dec}\approx 1100$, and usually are treated as happening the same timie as [[Recombination]].
