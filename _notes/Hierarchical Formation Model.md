At late times, perturbations becomoe nonlinear $\delta >1$.

In terms of power spectrum:
- Smaller Objects (large $k$) of [[Matter Power Spectrum]] form Earlier and become nonlinear.
- Larger Objects form from the merger events from smaller objects.

The [[Cosmic Web]] structure is formed under nonlinear evolutions.

Methods:
- Numerical simulations e.g. [[LSSIDE todo]].
- **Higher Order Perturbation** theory (small scales are just not perturbative at all)
- Simplified models: e.g. [[DM halo]] models.
