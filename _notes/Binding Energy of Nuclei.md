Binding energy of atoms:

$$
B_N = Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n-M_N
$$
denotes the aount of energy released by the formation of a nucleus N from scratch.

From the Binidng Energy diagram, we know that the Fe atom is the most stable, but in the context of [[Big-bang Nucleosynthesis]], the energy barrier before iron cannot be overcome due to low density.
