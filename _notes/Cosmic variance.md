The Cosmic Variance referes to the measurements for the largest scales of the universe in Either [[Power Spectrum]] or [[Angular Power Spectrum]]s such as **CMB** and **Matter Power Spectrum**. These measurements are subject to very large error bars, and this is because there just aren't enough samples for a scale that large.
