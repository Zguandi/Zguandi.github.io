A very mysterious part of the Standard model and boyond. We know that neutrinos have mass (very low mass), but we don; know its value. It is regarded as Ultrareletivistic in the [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]].

## What we know about neutrinos
- Has 3 generations.
- Has 2 polarizations $\nu , \bar{\nu}$.
- $\sum_i^3m_i>0.06eV$.
- Very weakly interacting with baryons.
- Are Fermionic.

> [!important] The newest results from DESI and ACT
> The cosmological probes for neutrinos gives prediction of $\sum_i m_i \lesssim 0.07eV$, which is kind of problematic.

Fermionic degree of freedom:
$$
\rho(T)=\int\frac{dp^3}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{p}{e^{P/T}+1}=\frac{7}{8}\rho_{\gamma}
$$
This means every fermionic degree of freedom has Energy $\rho_\nu = \frac{7}{8}\rho_\gamma$.

## Total degrees of freedom
$$
2[polarizations]\times 3[generations] =6
$$
# Relativistic to Nonrelativistic
At $k_BT\gg m_\nu$, the energy density scale $a^{-4}$.
At $k_BT<m_\nu$, the energy density of neutrinos scale as $a^{-3}$.

At today, the energy density of neutrinos are (by calculation):
$$
\Omega_\nu h^2=\frac{\sum_{m_{\nu_i}}}{94eV}
$$
Since $\Omega_\nu h^2<1$, this gives a bound on *Neutrino Mass*(A first bound on particle physics coming from cosmology). $\sum_i m_{\nu_i}<94eV$.

> [!NOTE] Bounds on Neutrinos today
> Coming from [[Cosmic Microwave Background]], and [[Large Scale Structure]] measurements, we have a better bound on neutrino mass,
> $$
> \sum_i m_{\nu_i}<0.2eV
> $$
