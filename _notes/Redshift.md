Suppose a photon is emmited at time t with [[Scale factor]] $a_{emit}$.

Define the redshift $z$ as:

$$
1+z=\frac{\lambda_{obs}}{\lambda_{emit}}=\frac{a_0}{a_{emit}}
$$
In an expanding universe, $\lambda \propto a$.

Can also be caused by [[Peculiar Velocity]] of the source (as a result of Doppler effect).
