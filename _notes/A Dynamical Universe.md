After [[Einstein's Equation]] and the invention of GR, people began to realize that is is very hard to have a *Static* Universe.

The most likely solution for the [[Einstein's Equation]] gives a [[Metric]] that is evolving with time.

In the 1930s, **Erwin Hubble** discovered the **Hubble Law** of Exapnsion.

Then there is a model of universe where the universe start from a *Hot, Dense* State.

In order for the universe to look like its present state [[Key observations of Cosmology]], we need all of the [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]]:
- [[Dark Matter]]
- [[Dark Energy]]
- [[Inflation theory]]
