Absolute magnitude is the [[Magnitudes]] of a star *If it is positioned* at a 10pc distance from us.

$$
m-M=2.5
log \frac{F}{F_{10pc}}=5\log \frac{d_L}{10pc}
$$

Where $d_L$ is the [[Luminosity distance]].

Absolute magnitude is equivalent to [[Absolute Luminosity]] as a property of intricis brightness.
