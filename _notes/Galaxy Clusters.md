The galaxy clusters are clusters of galaxies usually in the number of $\sim50,000$. The clusters usually have a radius of $\sim Mpc$. usually have a mass $\sim 10^{14}M_{\odot}$.

# Examples of clusters

- Abell cluster catalog (1958)
- Coma cluster
- Virgo Cluster

# Galaxy population of clusters

Since clusters are dense regions in galaxies and contains primarily early-type red galaxies.

> [!note] Astronomical dialect: Field
> Usually a galaxy not within a cluster is referred to be in a *Field*. The galaxies in the field has a less fraction of the early-type galaxies.


# Radial number density of galaxy clusters:
