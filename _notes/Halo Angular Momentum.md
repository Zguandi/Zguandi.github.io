Halos generally have angular momentum that is acquired as a composite result of the [[Large Scale Structure|LSS]] tidal field and the [[Merger Tree]].

A dimensionless parameter $\lambda = \frac{J|E|^{1/2}}{G M_{vir}^{5/2}}$ is usually used for charaterization of how much the halo is **Rotation Supported** vs **Random Motion Supported** (i.e. [[Virialisation]]).

A typical value of $\lambda$ is $\sim 0.03$.
