# Hypothesis-Super Massive Black Holes (SMBH)

Usually the AGN is hypothesized to be a central SMBH usually with a mass of $10^8\sim 10^9 M_\odot$.
The luminosity is at most the [[Eddington Luminosity]].

> [!tip] Anisotopy of Luminosity
> In most AGNs, the radiation is nowhere near isotropic and usualy accretion comes from the accretion disk (so does most of the radiation). This could change the overall mass-Luminosity relation of the SMBH, but the scaling relation is still the same as the [[Eddington Luminosity|Eddington Limit]].

Most galaxies has SMBH at centre but not all are *Active*.
