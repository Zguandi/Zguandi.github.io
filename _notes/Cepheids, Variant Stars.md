**Cepheids and RR Lyrae Stars** are all variant stars
## Cepheids
Cepheids have pulsation period related to its absolute magnitude. And can be used as standard scales in the distance ladder.

They are usually:
- High mass stars: M~3-50 M_sun, in terms of [[Stellar Populations]], they are Pop I stars.
- Have mass-period relation: $M_V=-2.0-2.8 \log P[d]$ , with Period ~ 3-40 days

## RR Lyrae Stars

They are usually:
- Low metallicity stars on Horizontal Branch(HB) on the [[HR-diagram]]. R~10R_sun.
- Pop II stars, very old. Pulsating due to *Opacity Effects*.
