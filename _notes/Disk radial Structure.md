# Dust disk inner edge
Inner edge of dusts usually ends and around 10 R_star. This is because the heat is enough to turn everything into gas state (inner most of [[Condensation Sequence]]).

# Gas Disk inner radius
Ther is a cavity between star and inner gas disk, where all material is completely [[ionizeation]], this is due to the magnetic field of the star. (size depends on magnetic field). The materials feed into [[magnetospheric accretion]] of the star.

has temperature ~ 10,000 K.

# Radial temeprature structure
for thin, flat disks, $T\propto r^{-3/4}$, and $L_{disk}=\frac{1}{4}L_*$
for flared disks, $T\propto r^{-1/2}$
