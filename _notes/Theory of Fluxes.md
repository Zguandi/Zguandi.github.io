The observed flux can be taken as:

$$
f_\lambda(d)=\frac{F_{\lambda}}{4\pi d^4} e^{-\tau_\lambda}
$$
Where $F_{\lambda}$ is the stellar output at $\lambda$ wavelength, and the $\tau_\lambda$ is the optical depth, denoting the extinction of photons due to *Absorption* and *Scattering*.

The relation between [[Magnitudes]] and [[Absolute magnitude]] is:
$$
m_{\lambda}=M_{\lambda}+5log(d/10pc)+A_\lambda
$$.
