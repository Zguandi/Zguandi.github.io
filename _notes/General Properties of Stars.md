# Radiation

Usually stars can be well approximated by a blackbody SED.

## Scaling relations

Stefan-Boltzmann: $L=4\pi R^2 \sigma T^4$, where $\sigma$ is the **Stefan Boltzman** Cosntant.
Wein's Law: $\lambda_{max}\cdot T_{eff}=2897.9 \mu m\cdot K$.
Luminosity appoximate law: $L\sim M^3$
# Mass vs evolution
The star properties are very much deterimined by the Mass. 

- Determines [[Spectral Types]]
- Determines Internal Energy transport (Convection vs Radiation)
- Determines Fusion Reaction

The stellar evolution is stated in the [[HR-diagram]]

To study the stellar evolution, we can use the [[Initial Mass Function]].
