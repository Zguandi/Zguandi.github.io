For discussion of matter components in the univserse, it is usefull to define the species specific ratio:
$$
\Omega_s = \frac{\rho_{s,0}}{\rho_{crit,0}}
$$
From [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]], we know the components has density scaling as
$$
\rho_s(a) = \rho_{s,0}(a_0/a)^{3(1+w)}
$$
For different components, there [[Distribution Function]] wil also evolve with a.
