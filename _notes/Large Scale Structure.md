# Linear regime of perturbations, horizon crossing

For long modes that stays outside the horizon $aH$, the modes stay constant be fore and after [[Matter-Radiation Equality]], except a decay of $10\%$. For a generic mode, the evolution is depicted by the [[Transfer Function]].

![Linear_perturbation_matter](/assets/pictures/Linear_perturbation_matter.png)
$$
\Phi(k,a)=\frac{3}{5}\zeta(k)\times T(k,a)
$$
# Nonlineararity

Initially the perturbations of DM is all linear, up untill around $z=20$, however the fluctuation becomes $\delta \sim 1$ afterwards, and perturbation ansatz break down.

At large scales $k<k_{NL}$ perturbations are linear, and this determines the locations of the LSS, we define the NL scale by looking at [[Matter Power Spectrum]].
