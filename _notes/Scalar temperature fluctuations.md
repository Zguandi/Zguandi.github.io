The [[CMB Power Spectrum]] has its initial conditions from [[Inflation theory]], and the [[Primordial scalar perturbations]], in particular.

The scalar perturbation only gives a perturbation field in **spatially flat gauge** during inflation, by the temperature fluctuation is not directly the $\delta \phi$ during inflation, but the result of the **reheating process** after the inflation.

![slow_roll_exit_inflation](/assets/pictures/slow_roll_exit_inflation.png)

# The Physics Picture
The initial condition for the [[Photon Perturbation]] is converted from the [[Scalar temperature fluctuations]]. A easy way of thinking about this is through the difference of inflation exit time at different spatial coordinates.

$$
H\frac{\delta \phi}{\phi}=\frac{H\delta t_{esc}}{a_{esc}}\sim\frac{\delta\rho}{\rho}=4\frac{\delta T}{T}
$$
This connects the $\zeta=H\frac{\delta \phi}{\phi}$ parameter with the CMB Temeperature fluctuation.
