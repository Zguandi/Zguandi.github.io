Jeans stability is the stability of molecular clouds that results in [[Star formation]].

The set of equations are:

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dt}(\frac{P}{\rho ^\gamma})&=0\\
\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}+\nabla \cdot(\rho v)&=0\\
\frac{\partial v}{\partial t}
\end{align}
$$

Predictions:
1. On large scales, the cloud exponentially collapse.
2. On small scales, the cloud have Acoustic Oscillations just like [[Baryon Acoustic Oscillation]].
