Planet Formation takes place in [[Protoplanetary Disks]]

## When do planets form
Need dust (+gas for giants), low enough temperature because of [[Condensation Sequence]] . ([[Spectral Energy Density and  disk evolution]] to at least Class I and Class II). This means usually planet formation starts in the first million year after star formation.

# planet accretion
planets formation usually takes the [[Boundary layer accretion]] regime.
