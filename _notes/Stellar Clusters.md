- Gravitationally Bound.
- Know about $\sim 1000$ of them.
- Estimated as $\sim 100000$ in the Milky Way.
- Stellar density $\sim 1 star/pc^3$.
- Often show mass segregation (Center stars are more massive).

We can determine a galaxy cluster using the *Turn-off stars*. If a certain spectal type of star exist, the galaxy cluster should be younger than the life span of that star.


> [!QUESTION] What if these are second round or third round forming stars
> Actually there exists a 'second batch' of stars in clusters. The real issue is more complicated.
