# The Luminosity function

$\phi(L)dL$=Number density of galaxies with [[Absolute Luminosity]] between $L,L+dL$.

The Schechter function is a good fit to most galaxy population:
$$
\phi(L)=\Phi^*(\frac{L}{L^*})^\alpha\exp(-\frac{L}{L^*})\frac{dL}{L^*}
$$
The color distribution has a **Binomial distribution** with a Red sequence and a Blue sequence (also seen in [[unWISE samples]])

![binomial_distribution_galaxies.png](/assets/pictures/binomial_distribution_galaxies.png.png)

There is a correlation between galaxy morphology with galaxy color: **Spiral** galaxies are more *Red* and **Elliptical** galaxies are more blue. Also there Redder galaxies cluster with each other more than blue galaxies.
