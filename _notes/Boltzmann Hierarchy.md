We approximate the photon distribution as $\Theta_0,\Theta_1$ and neutrino distributions $N_0,N_1$.

# Radiation dominant early times

The baryon-to-photon ratio $\eta_b = \frac{n_b}{b_\gamma}\ll1$, and the scattering $\tau'\gg H$.The baryons and photons are very tightly coupled.

The baryon moments:
$$
u_b'+\frac{a'}{a}u_b=-i\Psi+\frac{\tau'}{\eta_b}[u_b+3i\Theta_1]
$$
In tight-coupling, $u_b=-3i\Theta_1+\frac{R}{\tau'}\Theta$.

The photon equation:
$$
\Theta' +ik\mu\Theta= -\Phi'-ik\mu\Psi-\tau'[\Theta_0-\underbrace{(\Theta_0-3i\Theta_1\mu)}_{\Theta}+\mu \underbrace{u_b}_{-3i\Theta_1+\frac{R}{\tau'}\Theta}]
$$

This eventually gives:

$$
\Theta'+ik\mu \Theta = -\Phi'=ik\mu\Psi +\underbrace{\Theta R}_{small}
$$
> [!question]  Theta_0 -3i Theta1 mu= Theta?

Take moments of the equation:
$$
\int \frac{d\mu}{2} \Rightarrow\Theta_0+k\Theta_1=-\Phi'
$$

# Matter domination epoch

The matter perturbation:
$$
\begin{align}
\delta_c'+ik\mu_c&=-3\Psi'\\
u_c'+\frac{a'}{a}u_c&=ik\Phi
\end{align}
$$
Also the [[Einstein'e Equation perturbation]]:
$$
k^2\Phi = 4\pi G[\rho_c\delta_c+4\rho_\gamma \Theta_{\gamma,0}+\frac{3aH}{K}(i\rho_cu_c+4\rho_{\gamma})\Theta_{\gamma,1}]
