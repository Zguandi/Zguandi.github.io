In search for [[Dark Matter]] candidates like [[WIMPs]] and others we search for decay channels of $XX=\Psi\Psi$. looking into different directions in the Feynman diagram, we can detect the DM in *Direct* and *indirect* methods.

## Indirect measurements

measuring decay of DM self interaction that produces photons. We can look for gamma rays emitted in missions such as [Fermi](https://fermi.gsfc.nasa.gov/).

## Direct measurements

Measuring the *Elastic scattering* between DM-SM particles can give bounds on DM. This is greatly supressing models like [[WIMPs]].
