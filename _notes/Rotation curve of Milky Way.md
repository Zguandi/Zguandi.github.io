Using measurements of 21-cm line for the [[Measuremnets of ISM]], we can map the velocity of [[Interstellar Medium (ISM)]] within the Milky Way.
$$
v_r^{H_I-21cm}=(\Omega(R)-\Omega_0)R_0 \sin (l)
$$
Where l is galactic logtitude from [[Galactic Coordinates]].

The result gives a flat rotation curve for our galaxy.

This is also a indirect stopport for existence of [[Dark Matter]].
