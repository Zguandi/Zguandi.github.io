# Big Bang Scheme
We usually agree that the universe starts in a *Hot,Dense* State. After the big bang, a number of phase transitions happen as seen below.
# Thermal Equillibrium
Generically, particle reactions would be in Equillibrium when the *Reaction rate* $\Gamma$ exceeds the [[Hubble parameter]] $H$. This comes frome the Collision term in the [[Boltzmann Equation]].

Generally:
- Earlier Times: $\Gamma>>H$ has Equilllibrium.
- Later Times: $\Gamma<H$, the relevant parties [[Freeze Out]].
# Important Thermal Transitions
1. $t=10^{-5}s$, Temeperature $T=3\times 10^{12} K$, Energy $E\sim 300 MeV$, the [[Quark-Hardron Transition]] happens, baryons form from quarks, also know as **QCD Phase Transition**.
2. $t=4s$, Temeperature $T=5\times 10^{9} K$, Energy $E\sim 5 MeV$, $e^+,e^-$ annihilation, and $\nu,\bar{\nu}$ decouple.
3. $t\sim 1mins$, Temeperature $T=3\times 10^{9} K$, Energy $E\sim MeV$, [[Big-bang Nucleosynthesis]] happens, and n,p form nuclei (D,H,Li,...).
4. $t=380000yr$, Temeperature $T=4000 K$, Energy $E\sim eV$,[[Recombination]] happens, and photons decouple from the hot plasma, and universe became transparent. This remanent photons became the [[Cosmic Microwave Background]].
