There are two(three) phases of growth:
1. **orderly growth**: mass growth, but mass spectrum keeps the same (fractal)
2. **runaway growth**: (exponential), ceases at the point when the viscous stirring of the largest body exceeds the amount by the rest of planetesimals.
3. **origarchic growth**: (a small number of objects grow quickly while others don't)

This initial stage happens relatively quickly (0.01-1Myr). After that the assumption that each oligarch evolve independently breaks down. they interact strongly, collide and scatter. The final stage is slowest (~10Myr).
