Before talking about the stellar dynamics, there are sume scales we need to know: [[Velocity scales of stars]].

Celectrial objects has motion speed:
$$
v_s=\sqrt{v_t^2+v_r^2}
$$
we can measure the $v_r$ using [[Redshift]] of peculiar motion, and measure $v_t$ using [[Parallax]] motion.

We can measure the apparent motion, and below is the stellar trace observed by hippacus telescope.

![Parrlax_raw_observation](/assets/pictures/Parrlax_raw_observation.png)

But to interpret the motion (velocities), we need [[Galactic Coordinates]] (l,b) instead of the [[Equatorial Coordinates]]($\alpha, \delta$), and usually with reference to the [[Local Standard of Rest]](LSR).

In the LSR, we measure three components of the velocity: $u,v,w$
And the [[Velocity dispersion]] measured in the three components have relations:
$$
\langle u^2\rangle^{1/2}>\langle v^2\rangle^{1/2}>\langle w^2\rangle^{1/2}
$$
This is a hint that radially is *not a rigit body*.
