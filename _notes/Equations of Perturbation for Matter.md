For solving the fields of [[Cosmological Perturbation]], we need:
## Continuity Equation
$$
\frac{D\rho}{Dt}+\rho\nabla_r \cdot u=0
$$
Where $\frac{D}{Dt} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}+u\cdot \nabla$ is the **Convective Derivative**.
## Euler Equation
$$
\frac{Du}{Dt}=-\frac{\nabla_r P}{\rho}-\nabla_r\Phi
$$
This is the equation of motion for the fluid.
## Poisson Equation
$$
\nabla^2_r\Phi = 4\pi G \rho
$$
Is the Equation for gravitational potential.

> [!NOTE] The nablas and the velocity
> The velocity u and the nablas are all defined as the derivative of/by the **Physical Distance** r, as opposed to the comoving coodinates x.
