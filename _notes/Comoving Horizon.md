Consider a photon starting travelling at $t=0$ (Singularity), following the [[Geodesic of Photons in FLRW]].

The [[Comoving distance]] of that photon denotes the maximum comivong coordinates a photon can traverse in the universe.

$$
\chi_h=\frac{c}{H_0}\int\frac{da}{a^2H(a)^2}
$$
Using the [[Friedmann Equations]], we get:
$$
\chi_h=\frac{c}{H_0}\int\frac{da}{a^2}[\Omega_{\Lambda,0}+\Omega_{m,0}(a_0/a)^3+\Omega_{r,0}(a_0/a)^4+\Omega_{k,0}(a_0/a)^2]^{-1/2}
$$
After integration, this yields a value of $\chi_h\approx 14.4 Gpc$, at the same scale of the $\frac{c}{H_0}=3000h^{-1}Mpc$, or the [[Sound Horizon]].
