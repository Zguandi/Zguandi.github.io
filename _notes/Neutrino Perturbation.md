In the homogeneous limit if [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]], [[Neutrino]]s are described by the F-D distribution with $T_{\nu}\propto a^{-1}$. Similar to [[Photon Perturbation]], we can parametrize as $f_{\nu}(x,p,t)=\left[ \exp(p/T_\nu(x,p,t))-1 \right]^{-1}$. And temperature $T_\nu = T_\nu(a)(1+\mathcal{N}(x,p,t))$.

After [[Freeze Out]] of neutrinos, $C[f]=0$.
$$
\begin{align}
0&=\partial_t f_\nu +\frac{p}{\epsilon_\nu(p)}\frac{\hat{p}^i}{a}\partial_i \mathcal{N}-Hp \partial_p \mathcal{N}+\dot{\Phi}+\frac{\epsilon_nu(p)}{ap}\hat{p}^i \partial_i \Psi
\end{align}
$$
