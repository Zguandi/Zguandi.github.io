From Discussions in [[Baryons]], we know that baryons have a same backgorund scalaing as [[Dark Matter]], and are subdominant in overall mass ratio:
$$
\Omega_{b}=0.05,\Omega_m=0.25
$$
However, baryons makes up for most of the astronomical observations because only baryons could emit light.

# Phases of Baryons
There is a gas cycle in the evolution of baryons that involves phases:
1. Gas: hot gas (ionized)/ cold gas (atomic or molecular),
2. Stars: Central dense objects after [[Core accretion]].
# Distribution of Baryons
- Within galaxies: [[Inter-Stellar-Medium (ISM)]]
- Between galaxies: [[Intergalactic Medium (IGM)]]
- Within galaxy clusters: [[Intra-Cluster Medium (ICM)]]
