## Asteroids

Rocky, and usually not spherical bodies, in e.g. Asteroid belts.

3 types:
- C-type : dark carbonaceous objects (75% of known asteroids)
- S-type : Stoney (sillicaceous) objects (17% of known asteroids)
- X-type: metalic

### Condrites
Stoney meterite that has not been modified (either melting or differentiation). Contain chondrules (mm-sized grains, remains of the original planet formation process)
- They make up 86 % of all meteorites
- Water in form of hydrated minerals
- Calcium-Aluminum Rich (CAI) inclusions.=> dating of solar system.

# CAI

The Aluminium has decay channel: $^{26}Al\rightarrow ^{26}Mg$.
$$
\begin{align}
(^{26}Mg)=\ ^{26}Mg|_{t_0}+\ ^{26}Al(1-e^{-t/\tau})
\end{align}
$$
Introducing the two stable versions:
$$
(\frac{^{26}Mg}{^{24}Mg})=\frac{^{26}Mg}{^{24}Mg}|_{t_0}+\underbrace{(\frac{^{27}Al}{^{24}Mg})}_{constant}(\frac{^{26}Al}{^{27}Al})(1-e^{-t/\tau})
$$
The concentration of $^{26}Mg$ has relation:
$$
\delta^{26}=a+(\frac{^{26}Al}{^{27}Al})\times b(t)
$$
Doing a survey of a population of CAIs gives a scatter that reveals the CAI.
