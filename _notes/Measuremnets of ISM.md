For ISM *near the sun*, can measure via **Absoption lines** to background stars.
- molecular gas: H2, CO(UV), CH (optics)
- atomic gas: HI-Lymann, C I, C II, Si II, ...(far UV) Ca II, Na I (UV)
- photonized gas: C IV, Si III, S IV (far UV)
- collision ionized gas: O VI (far UV)

Gives information for the components of [[Interstellar Medium (ISM)]]

The measurement gives the result that we, solar system, lives in [[The Local Bubble]].

For ISM *further away* from the sun,  we can measure the 21-cm line or the CO 2.6 mm and 1.3 mm lines.
- 21-cm line, optically thin, not absorped by dust
- CO abundant, but not that optically thin,
- C13O, not abundant but optically thin.
