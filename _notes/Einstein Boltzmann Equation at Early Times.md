Using [[Cosmological Initial Conditions]]:

We can set the initial conditions for [[Photon Perturbation]] in terms of $R$ or $\Psi$ because there is dependence on observables during inflation.

To solve this we are doing the equaivalent of [[Photon Perturbation]], with the only difference being the metric is in inflation.
# Photon Sector
The Photon Boltzman Equation:
$$
\Theta'+ik\mu \Theta=-\Phi'-ik\mu\Psi-\tau'[\Theta_0-\Theta+\mu\mu_b-\frac{1}{2}P_2\Pi]
$$
At long wavelengths and early times $\Theta'<\theta/\eta\gg k\mu\Theta$.
$$
\Phi'\gg k\mu \Psi
$$
The Equations can be simplified as $\theta'=-\Phi'-\tau'[\Theta_0-\Theta+\mu\mu_b]$.

For Velocity perturbation:
$$
u_b+\frac{a'}{a}\mu_b=-ik\Psi+\frac{\tau'}{R}[\mu_b+3i\Theta_1]
$$
At Early times $\tau'/R$ is very large because curvature is small, and collision cross section is large.
