The hill sphere is the shpere where the protoplanet has gravity dominance over the star.
$$
r_H = a \sqrt{\frac{M_p}{3M_*}}
$$
the hill velocity is the orbital velocity around the protoplanet at the hill sphere boundary. The hill velocity can be done by taylor expansion of $\sqrt{\frac{GM}{R}}$ and yields $v_H=\frac{1}{2}\Omega r_H$. for a deviation of $\Delta a$, this $v_{rel}=\frac{1}{2}\Omega \Delta a$

The [[Gravitational focusing]] factor which depend on
$$
\frac{v_{esc}^2}{v_{rel}^2}=\frac{r_H^3}{R\Delta a^2}=(\frac{r_H}{\Delta a^2})^3\frac{r_H}{R_p}
$$

The planet **feeding zone** usually has $r=2.3r_H$. Planets with separation $<3r_H$ usually collide, beyond this usually won't.
