A very Specific and *Important* example for the [[Perturbation Fields]].

A field is **Gaussian** if for any given set of points $x_1,x_2,x_3,...x_N$, the joint distribution:
$$
Prob(\delta(x_1),\delta(x_2),....\delta(x_3))
$$
is a Joint Gaussian distribution.

It can be proven that, for a gaussian field, the full statistics is given by its 2-pt function $\xi(r)$ or $P(k)$. This is the [Wick's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wick%27s_theorem).

## Application in cosmology

- Early Times: Perturbation fields are gaussian
- late times: as long as it is in linear regime, fields are gaussian. Non-Linear scales are non-gaussian.
