Consider a fully ionized plasma (Like Hot gas in the ICM), electrons and protons collide:
$$
p+e^-\rightarrow p+e^-+\gamma
$$
Assume the electrons and protons are at thermal distribution of $T, the emmisivity
$$
\epsilon(\nu)\propto n_i n_e g(\nu,T)T^{-1/2}\exp(-\frac{h\nu}{k_BT})
$$
