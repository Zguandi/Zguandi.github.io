The relaxation times defines the timescale when $\delta v^2 \sim v^2$
$t_{relax}\approx n_{relax} \cdot t_{cross}\approx \frac{0.1 ln N}{N}.$

This is the timescale is the typical time for a particle cloud with a velocity distribution to evolve into another (meaning [[Virialisation]]) velocity.
