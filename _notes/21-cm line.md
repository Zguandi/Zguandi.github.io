[Wiki Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_line). The 21-cm line is an extremely important tool for #Observational_cosmology .

# The line transition
The Transition from H I electron spin has a very low spontaneous excitation rate:
$$
A_{21}=3\times 10^{-15}s^{-1}
$$
Also the Number density of Hydrogen is aroun $N_H = 1 cm^{-3}$. These numbers are all extremely low.

The collision term $N_H Q = 10^{-9} s^{-1}$
