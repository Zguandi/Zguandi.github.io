# Early times (Equillibrium)

For Photons, they have [[Distribution Function]] obeying the Bose-Einstein distribution.

$$
p_\gamma=2\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{p}{e^{p/T}-1}=\frac{\pi^2}{15}T^4
$$
At z=0, the photon temperature is $T_{CMB}=2.72650K$  for phtons in the [[Cosmic Microwave Background]].

# Later times, after [[Freeze Out]]

Since p and T both has scaling with $a^{-1}$, even after the [[Freeze Out]], the distribution function still have the same function shape because $f=f(\frac{P}{T})$. This is a very special coincidence for photons.

In thr process of [[Freeze Out]] at phton docoupling event in [[A Thermal  History of the Universe]],
$$
f_{out}(\epsilon,T)=f_{BE}(\epsilon \frac{a_0}{a_{out}}/T_{out})=f_{BE}(\epsilon,\frac{T_{out}a_{out}}{a_0})
$$
