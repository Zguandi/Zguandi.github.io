The galaxy formation process is a very complex and intertwined process. Notonly the internal gas circulation matters, there are also external inflow and outflow of gas.

![galaxy_gas_cycle](/assets/pictures/galaxy_gas_cycle.png)

# The Gas circulation within galaxy

1. [[Gas Cooling]] plays an important part in doing phase transition from hot gas to cold gas, and helps star formation
2. Evolved stars give Supernova [[Galaxy Feedback]].
3. The centre [[AGN]] contributes to [[Galaxy Feedback]].


# Gas inflow and outflow

The galaxy feeds on baryon matter in the LSS.
