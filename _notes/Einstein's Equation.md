## Equations

The dynamics of spacetime is described by the **Einstein's Equation**.

$$
G_{\mu\nu}=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}
$$
Where $G_{\mu\nu}$ is the *Einstein tensor*.

And the $T_{\mu\nu}$ is the result from matter component, and is called the [[Energy Momentum tensor]]
