# Supernovae

Supernovas are the explosion caused by centre collapse of massive stars at the end of its life cycle.

# SN Ia

The SNIA is a **Standard Candle** for the [[Distance ladder]] and is used to callibrate the distnce measurements.

- This type of Supernovae produces "*iron peak*" elements (Cr, Fe, Ni, Co)
- Consists of a WD(white dwarf)-companion binary, where mass is stripped from the companion to the WD. The WD will trigger Supernovae explosion when $m=m_{crit}=1.4M_{\odot}$.

Observationally, the SNIa has different spectrum fron other Supernovae events.
