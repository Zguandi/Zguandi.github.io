Following the [[FLRW metric]], we can compute the Christoffel symbol using:
$$
\Gamma^\mu_{\rho\sigma}=\frac{1}{2}g^{\mu\nu}(g_{\nu\rho,\sigma}+g_{\nu\sigma,\rho}-g_{\rho\sigma,\nu})
$$
# Flat FLRW
The result for flat FLRW, which is most related to present universe, comes as:
$$
\Gamma^0_{00}=0,\Gamma^0_{0i}=0,\Gamma^{0}_{ij}=-\frac{1}{2}g^{00}g_{ij,0}=\delta_{ij}a\dot{a}
$$
