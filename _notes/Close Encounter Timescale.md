For a 2 body scattering in [[DM halo]] or [[Stellar Clusters]], there is a velocity exchange $\delta v$ between the two particles (which depends on the scattering parameter $b$).

We define a *Close Encounter* as an encounter that has $\delta v \sim \langle v \rangle$.

The *Close Encounter timescale* is defined as the average time between two close encounters.

It can be shown that:
$$
t_{close} \simeq N t_{cross}
$$
