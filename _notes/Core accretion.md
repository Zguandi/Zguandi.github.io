The core accretion model is a model for #planet_formation  that is building planets from dust. (another model is [[Core collapse]] for more preliminary discussion, but doesn't work for [[Minumum Mass Solar Nebular (MMSN)]] in order to reproduce solar system.)

The scales that is covered is drastic:
![planetesimals_scales](/assets/pictures/planetesimals_scales.png)

The *hydrostatic Equillibrium*:
$$\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial P}{\partial r}+\frac{GM}{r^2}=r\Omega^2
$$
There are different matter components:
1. Gas: $\frac{\partial P}{\partial r}<0$, has a density profile.
2. Solids: $P\sim0, \frac{\partial P}{\partial r}=0$.

This implies from equation (1) that the gas component has a $\Omega_{gas}<\Omega_{solid}$. This means there is a [[Aerodynamic drag]] acting on all solid particles. This makes solid particles **settle down fully** on the disk surface after drage timescale.

The decoupling happens if the Solids are massive enough when the drag is negligible.

The core accretion model hypothesizes that most solids concentrated on the disk  grow by colliding with each other (see [[Collision Physics]]).

The dust grow with the collisions from grains up untill planetesimal sizes. And steps into **Oligarchic Growth**.

Afterwards, it gains feeding from the planet's [[Feeding Zone]], and enters [[Terrestrial planet formtion]] or [[Giant Planet Formation]] depending on the location and disk properties.
