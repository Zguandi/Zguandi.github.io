Baryons are ususally referred to as the 'visible' part of the univserse, namely protons, neutrons and electrons, etc.

## Three ways of probing baryonic fraction
1. Astrophysical Measurment (hard, because most baryons dont emit light)
2. [[Cosmic Microwave Background]] and measuremnt of the [[Large Scale Structure]]
3. [[Big-bang Nucleosynthesis]] predictions.

It turns out that 2, and 3  agrees. under [[LCDM model]].
