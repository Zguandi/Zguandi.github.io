The virialized region of DM overdensity after nonlinear collapse, DM halos are vital components of the [[Large Scale Structure]].

# halo models

1. [[Top-hat collapse]], is the most simple model describing a overdense region of DM which is spherically symmetric.
2. [[Virialisation]] of halos.

# Statistic Propertiels of Halos

1. For given halo:
	1. Position, velocity
	2. Mass, virial radius (See [[NFW halo profile|NFW profile]])
	3. [[Halo Shape]], [[Halo Angular Momentum]].
	4. halo profiles parameters like [[NFW halo profile]]
	5. ...
2. For collection of halos:
	1. [[Halo mass function]].
	2. [[Merger Tree]].
		1. *Assembly history*: halo main progenitor mass evolution.
		2. *Assembly time*: Time for a halo to evolve to falf of its final mass.
		3. *Merger rate*: rate of a halo to merge and get mass $\Delta M$.
		4. *halo survival time*: Time for  given halo to evolve into double its mass,
		5. ...
	3. [[Halo Clustering]].
	4. [[Halo Subtructure]].
	5. ...
