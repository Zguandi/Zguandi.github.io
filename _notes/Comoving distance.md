Parent Catergory [[Cosmological Distances]]:

With Photons following null geodesic in the [[FLRW metric]], what is the amount of comoving coordinate the photon traverses?

$0=ds^2=c^2dt^2-a^2(t)d\chi^2$. And this yields,
$$
d\chi=\frac{c^2dt^2}{a^2(t)}
$$

Change variable to $a$ instead of t $dt=\frac{da}{aH}$:
$$
d\chi=\frac{cda}{a^2H(a)}
$$
This gives us a $\Delta\chi$ correspondance to the $a$ [[Scale factor]],
$$
\chi(a)=c\int_a^{a_0}\frac{da'}{a'^2H(a')}
$$
Note that:
- $H(a)$ is a **Model Dependent** function
- This Measurement of comoving distance using redshift only is subject to *peculiar velocity* Due to doppler effect.
