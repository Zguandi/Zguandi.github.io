The recombination happens when $T<T_{H}$, and is less than the binding energy of hydrogen atoms. The [[Freeze Out]] happens
$$
p+e\leftrightarrow \gamma + H
$$
The Recombination happens at $z_{rec}\approx 1300$.

![Recombination](/assets/pictures/Recombination.png)

Recombination happens at *around the same time* as [[Decoupling]], but recombination is slightly earlier.
