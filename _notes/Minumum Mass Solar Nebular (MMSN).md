**Lower Limit** of the [[solar system]]'s [[Protoplanetary Disks]]. This is derived by directly crunching the material of planets and smear around the planet feeding zone. Since the material planet efficiency is less than 100%, the MMSN should be a lower limit.

Total mass: 10 M_jup=0.01 M_sun
$$
\Sigma(r)=1.7\times10^3(r/AU)^{-3/2}g \cdot cm^{-2}
$$

Also another model considering the effect of the [[snow line]].

## Down side
- assume planets are born where they are today.
- density profile too steep, doesn't mathc observations
- very unrealistic, assumes 100% effiency
## Pro side
- Easiest way of representing a disk
- the total mass ~ 10 M_jup is correct
