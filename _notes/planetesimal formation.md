How to get over the barriers?
1. Self gravity ([[Toomre Q]] very low)
2. Dust-gas coupling
3. Instabilities (e.g. streaming instability directly form planetesimals)
4. Vorticies trapping solids to reach high local density

All these factors require high local density and/or low-velocity viscocity. The process could be:

- dust settrling in mid-plane+local dust traps
- creating high local density and ToomreQ<1
