The $R$ field is a Gaussian field With [[Power Spectrum]]: $P_R(k)=(2\pi)^2 A_s \frac{1}{k^3}(\frac{k}{k^*})^{n_s-1}$. When $n_s = 1$, the power spectrum is scale-invariant.

The Tensor perturbation: $h$ also has a power spectrum: $P_h(k)=\frac{\pi^2}{2}A_T \frac{1}{k^3}(\frac{k}{k^*}^{n_T})$, when $n_s = 0$, the power spectrum is scale-invariant.

The deviation from scale-invariant comes from the *slow-roll parameters* in [[Inflation theory]].

$$
P_R \propto \frac{1}{k^3}(\frac{k}{k^*})^{n_s-1}=\frac{H^4}{\dot{\phi}^2}|_{k\eta=-1}
$$
This gives $n_s-1 = -4\epsilon_{SR}-2\delta_{SR}$. (by some derivation)

The tensorial perturbations:
$$
P_{h}\propto \frac{1}{k^3}(\frac{k}{k^*})^{n_T}=\frac{H(\eta)^2}{M_{pl}^2}|_{k\eta=-1}
$$
This gives:
$$
n_T=-2\frac{d \ln H}{d \ln k}|_{k\eta=-1}=-2\epsilon_{SR}
$$
This gives the **initial power spectrum** that set stage for [[Einstein Boltzmann Equation at Early Times]], and eventually detectable in [[CMB Power Spectrum]], with [[Boltzmann Codes]] such as **CAMB** as models.
