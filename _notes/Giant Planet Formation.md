Giant planet formation is unlike the terrestrial planet formation. Instead od coagulation of solids, the gas giants also need to condensate gas. Giant planets form *outside* the [[snow line]].

There are tow models for the gas giant formation.
1. core accretion model (solid core builds up ~ 10 M_Earth, then  attrat gases). (bottom up scenario)
2. disk instability model (very heavy star >5M_jup), no solid core, forming like a star. (Top-down scenario)

In terms of [[Jupiter]], the gravitational probes has detected **NO** significant solid core, and people theorize that Jupiter might had collisions with other planets and the core became diffuse afterwards.
