The perturbation theory of [[Boltzmann Equation Perturbation]] applied for photons gives an prediction for the CMB anisotropy, and in other words, the [[CMB Power Spectrum]].

From the temeprature functuation $\Theta(x,\hat{p},t)$, we do fourier transfrom to get $\vec{k}$ conjugate to $\vec{x}$, and define the $\mu=\hat{k}\cdot \hat{p}$.

And we define the **Moments**:
$$
\Theta_l(k,t)=\frac{1}{(-i)^2}\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{d \mu}{2}P_l(\mu)\Theta(\vec{k},\mu,t)
$$
