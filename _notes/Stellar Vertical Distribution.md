Looking perpendicular to the [[Galactic Disk]], we have stellar distribution:
$$
N(z,S)=M(0,S)e^{-z/\beta_S}
$$
And the decay rate $\beta_S$ depends on the mass, the higher the mass/[[Spectral Types]], the smaller $\beta_S$ is, and the star is more confined around the centre disk.
