For a given system of planets, Lindblad resonances happens and there are certain orbits that have periodic excitation and enhancement (in contrast with stochastic quasi-periodic motion).

The lindblad resonance applied to a smoothed density predicts [[Planetary spiral wakes]] around forming planets, and this makes the [[Planet migration]] torque to have uneven contribution ($\Delta J_{in}<\Delta J_{out}$). This gives a planet a friction that makes the planets spiral inwards.

When the resonance is happening for the same orbit (m=0 case), the corotating physics is more complicated.
