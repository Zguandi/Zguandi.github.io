A critical part of the [[LCDM model]].

Has following properties c.f. observations:
- Cold == Non-relativistic Matter ([[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]]).
- Dark == Do not emit light and do not interact (greatly) with [Standard Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Model).
- Seemingly only interacts through Gravitation.

Also see [[WIMPs]] for an example. *Hot* DM has been ruled out by [[Large Scale Structure]].
## Properties
1. Interacts **Very Little** (if interact) with *itself* (Currently SIDM is very entangled with baryon feedback) and *baryons*.
## Observational Proofs
1. Galaxy rotation curves
2. [[Cosmic Microwave Background]]
3. [[Large Scale Structure]]
4. [[Bullet Clusters]], and proof crom lensing and X-ray experiments.

## Dark Matter Candidates

- Macroscopic candidates are mostly ruled out by [[Lensing]] experiments. (*MACHO*s)
- [[WIMPs]] are heavily constrained by direct detection.
- Anything Beyond SM could be a candidate.
