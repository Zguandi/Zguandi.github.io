The Milky Way has its own evolution in the context of the local [[DM halo]], which happens at cosmic timescales ~ 13Gyrs ago.

| Epoch                        |                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Early Universe               | First [[DM halo]]s, [[Big-bang Nucleosynthesis]] for H I and He                                                                      |
| high-z Reionization<br>Epoch | - Rapid growth of stars<br>- low [[Stellar Metallicity]] and (? high cooling)<br>- High [[Star formation rate]].                     |
| low-z Universe<br>z<1        | - galaxy structures are settled.<br>- [[Galactic Bulge]], gas/star [[Galactic Disk]], spiral structures form, see [[Schematic view of the Milky Way]]. |

The physics processes involved:
- Accretion history (mass + angular momentum).
- Radiation cooling + hydrodynamics of gas.
- Star formation, stellar nucleosynthesis.
- Stellar **feedback** and SN **feedback**.
- [[Stellar dynamics]]
