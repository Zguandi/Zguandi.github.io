Part of the [[Condensation Sequence]]. Meaning the line corresponds to water condensating into water ice (130K~170K).

In [[solar system]], the snow line is ~3AU, just between [[Mars]] and [[Jupiter]].

Beyond snowline,  [[Giant Planets]]form
Within Snowline, [[Terestrial Planets]] form

The snowline is an important division for planet forming efficiency. and [[Planetesimals]] coagulate more efficiently.

![Pasted image 20241211220521](/assets/pictures/Pasted image 20241211220521.png)

Inside the snowline, [[Planetesimals]] are built slower. Just over snowline is the quickest forming area.
