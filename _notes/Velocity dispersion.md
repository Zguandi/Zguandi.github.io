there are many competing effects affecting the velocity dispersion.
1. Viscous stirring. weak gravitational encounters can heat up a cold population of planetesimals. works with equal-sized objects.
2. Dynamical friction, happens only when there is a spectrum of masses. tend to develop a mass-independent dispersion of velocity. (energy equipartition)
3. Gas drag (much weaker for planetesimals), damps dispersion.
4. Inelastic collisions: results in energy dissipation.
5. turbulent stirring: excitation coming from turbulence of the disk.
