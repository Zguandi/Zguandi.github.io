The concordance model, with most observations 'agree' on this matter. Have raised two extra component of [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]] beyond the **Standard Model**.

Sometimes called **The Standard Model of Cosmology**.

The Ingredients are:
- [[Photons]], radiation;
- [[Neutrino]]s;
- [[Baryons]] from [[Big-bang Nucleosynthesis]]
- [[Dark Matter]];
- [[Dark Energy]].
See [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]].

> [!NOTE] The contribution of radiation and baryons by stars
> The contribution to $\Omega_r$ from stars $\Omega_r^*$ only.
> The contribution to $\Omega_b$ from *glowing* matter $\Omega_b^* \approx 0.0004\sim 0.1 \Omega_b$. This means that most baryons are in the form of *cold, warm and hot gases*, usually in the [[Intergalactic Medium (IGM)]].
