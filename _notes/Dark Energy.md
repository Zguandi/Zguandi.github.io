Also Known as [[Cosmological Constant]].

1998 Observation of distant Supernovae [[SNIa]], gives astonishing results saying that the universe has accelerating expansion.

Has very strange properties as a component in [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]].
- negative pressure $P=-\rho$. (For a general assumption, need $w<-\frac{1}{3}$).
- Current value at $\Omega_\Lambda\sim70\%$.

Makes up for the [[LCDM model]].

> [!NOTE] The Cosmological Constant Problem
> The theretical prediction of vacuum energy (Assuming Quantum Gravity at $k_{Planck}$), gives a $\rho_\Lambda = 10^{120} \rho_{\Lambda, observe}$. This would lead to a universe that **Double** size in every $10^{-40}s$.

## The Equation of State

From Second [[Friedmann Equations]], we get:
$$
\frac{\ddot{a}}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\rho+3P)
$$
However since the Universe is *Accelerating its expandsion*. This makes $3P<-\rho$, and the $w<-\frac{1}{3}$.

1. A [[Cosmological Constant]]. We might not know what it is, only a number in the [[Einstein's Equation]], will have strict $w=-1$.
2. [[Quintessence]] Model. (dynamical scalar fields.)
3. Modified Gravity theories ([[MOND]], etc.)
