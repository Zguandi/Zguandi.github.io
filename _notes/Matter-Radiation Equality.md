The transition between the [[Photons]] dominated radiation era to [[Dark Matter]], [[Baryons]].

For [[Photons]], according to [[Background matter scaling, Fluid Equations]]:
$$
\frac{\rho_\gamma}{\rho_{crit}}=\frac{2.47\times 10^-5}{h^2}\frac{(1+3\cdot\frac{7}{8}(\frac{4}{11})^{\frac{4}{3}})}{a^4}
$$
For [[Dark Matter]] and [[Baryons]], 
$$
\frac{\rho_m}{\rho_{crit}}=\Omega_m a^{-3}
$$
The [[Scale factor]] for [[Matter-Radiation Equality]] is:
$$
a_{eq}\approx 3300^{-1}
$$
Also see [[A Thermal  History of the Universe]]. Note that only at around $z=1100$, [[Recombination]] happens, that is because the Photon/baryon ratio is very high.
