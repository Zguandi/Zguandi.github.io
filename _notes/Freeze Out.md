As Expansion happens, the interaction between particles have decreasing reaction rates (typically $\propto \rho^2$)

When 
$$
\frac{m\sigma v}{H}\sim\frac{mean free path}{H}<<1
$$
The reaction halts, and the involved particles are decoupled.

The Effect of freeze out on [[Distribution Function]] is that the distribution function is 'frozen-in' and kept the same shape with the decoupling time (Only up to rescaling). Also seen in section [[Photons]].

$$
f_{after}(p,t)=f(p(\frac{a_f}{a(t)})^k,t_f)
$$
