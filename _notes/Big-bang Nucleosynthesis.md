In the past when $T>Mev$, photons, Neutrons, Neutrinos, etc are enegetic enough to destroy any neuclei that forms.

As expansion, the temeprature goes down, and the nuclei that get formed gets frozen to the equillibrium value at $T_{freezeout}$.

This gives the prediction of relative densities of neuclei. H, D, ...

This could be compared to the baryon fraction given by the CMB.

## Electron positron annihilation
At around 1MeV, The photons and electron-positrons are in equillibrium.
$$
\begin{gather}
e^++e^-\leftrightarrow\gamma+\gamma \\
e^++\gamma\rightarrow e^++\gamma+\gamma
\end{gather}
$$
Before the 1MeV level, the protons and neutrons has annihilated with the respective antiparticle.
$$
\frac{N_b}{s}=\frac{n_b-n_{\bar{b}}}{s}
$$
is conserved (number conservation). (Listing the entropy s here is because $s=\frac{\rho+P}{T}$ and is the indicator of a photon number because it is a radiation dominated epoch).
## Initial conditions
At early times when $T\gg 0.8MeV$, protons and neutrons are in [[Particle Equillibrium]] through *weak interaction* channels:
$$
p+e\leftrightarrow n+\nu_e
$$
and 
$$
p+\bar{e}\leftrightarrow p+\bar{\nu_e}
$$
> [!NOTE] The protons are non-relativistic
> Mass of p and n are at $938MeV$, which becomes non-relatvistic at $\sim 10^{-6}s$ wit $T>10^{13}K$.

Since $m_n-m_p=1.2MeV$, the two components freeze out at $\frac{m_n}{m_p}=0.2$, and due to *Weak Interaction* beta decay, the neutron fraction keeps decaying.

## Nuclear reaction chains

There is a chain of reactions peaking at $t=300s$,$T=0.8\times10^9K,E=0.07MeV$.
The initial BBN can only form lighter elements, see [[Binding Energy of Nuclei]].
![Nuclearsynthesis_channels](/assets/pictures/Nuclearsynthesis_channels.png)

This gives a prediction of particle number density as a function of the *Baryon to photon* ratio $\eta=\frac{n_b}{n_\gamma}$. The observation of atom abundances gives prediction of $\eta\approx6\times 10^{-10}$, and this result agrees very well with the [[Cosmic Microwave Background]] with [[LCDM model]].
