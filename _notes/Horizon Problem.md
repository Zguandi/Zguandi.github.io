The **Horizon Problem** is basically the motivation for the [[Inflation theory]]. (Also see [[Flatness Problem]])

Recall [[Particle horizon]] or [[Comoving Horizon]]. We can calculate the comoxing horizon at the point of [[Recombination]]. This radius $\chi_{rec}$ corresponds to the scale that has communicated since the **Big Bang** (if there is nothing before the big bang). The angular scale is: $\Delta \theta = 1.8\degree$

$$
\Delta \theta = \frac{\chi_h}{d_A}=\frac{\frac{c}{H_0} \frac{1}{1100}\times 6\times 10^{-5}}{12.673 Mpc}
$$

Right from Earth, we see that the [[Cosmic Microwave Background]] have very similar temperature even though they are observed at large angular separations. It turns out that these points are outside the comoving horizon, thus having *no causal connection*.

![CMB_horizon_probem_fig1](/assets/pictures/CMB_horizon_probem_fig1.png)
On the space-time diagram, the CMB photons aren't in the same light cone and should not have any causal relation. We had expected the universe to me more anisotropic in this senario.
![CMB_horizon_problem_fig2](/assets/pictures/CMB_horizon_problem_fig2.png)

However , if we plug in a initial fast-expansion phase such as [[Inflation theory]].

![CMB_horizon_problem_fig3](/assets/pictures/CMB_horizon_problem_fig3.png)

The **Past lightcone** could be totally included by the **Future light cone** with scale of [[Comoving Horizon]].
