The FLRW metric is the only possible solution for a homogeneous and isotropic universe for the [[Einstein's Equation]].

$$
ds^2=c^2dt^2-a^2(t)[d\chi^2+r^2(\chi)(d\theta^2+sin^2\theta d\phi^2)]
$$
The coordinate $\chi$ is correspinding to the radius in the usual sense spherical coordinates.

The $r(\chi)$ function is dependent on the geometry of the spacetime.

$$
r(\chi)=\cases{\sin\chi &(K=+1)\\\chi &(K=0)\\\sinh\chi &(K=-1)}
$$

And the function $a(t)$ is the [[Scale factor]] tha describes the contraction and expansion of the universe.

## Case K=0

$$
ds^2 = c^2dt^2-a^2(t)[d\chi^2+\chi^2(d\theta^2+sin^2\theta d\phi^2)] \\
     = c^2dt^2-a^2(t)(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)
$$

the spacetime is spatially flat, only contracting.
The spatial topology $\Sigma = \mathbb{R}^3$

The Universe is *to very good approximation*, spatially flat.

## Case K=+1

Also called *Closed Spcae*. Space has geometry of a 3D sphere (maybe embedded in a 4D Eucleadian space)

The spatial topology $\Sigma = \mathcal{S}^3$.

## Case K=-1

Also called *Open Case*. The space is hyperbolic at any time slice.
The spatial topology $\Sigma = \mathcal{H}^3$.
