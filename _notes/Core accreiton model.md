Three phases of core accretion  model
1. coagulation of solid core
2. Slow envelope accretion (ends at m_sold = m_gas) Hydrostatic growth
3. runaway growth, hydrostatic equi broke, gas compress itself. 10^4-10^5 years, but gains most of its mass

! gap opening usually do't stop runaway growth, because the accretion happens by meridional accretion. The photoevap happens at ~ 3Myr, meaning there is time to build super giants.
