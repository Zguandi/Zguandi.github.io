In linear growth, $\delta(a)\propto D(a)$ separates out the time dependence of the overdensity field. 

The logarithmic growth rate $f$ is defined as:
$$
f = \frac{d \ln D(a)}{d \ln a}
$$
Such that:
$$
\dot{D}=HDf; \dot{\delta}=H\delta f
$$
This expression is important in Derivation of [[Peculiar Velocity]] and [[Galaxy Clutering]].
