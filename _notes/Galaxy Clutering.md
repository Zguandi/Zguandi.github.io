Measurements of Galaxy Clustering can be used to probe [[Large Scale Structure]] and the [[Matter Power Spectrum]]

- Theory predicts the distribution of DM perturbation ($\delta$)
- Observation only probe visible baryons.

This Introduces the concept of [[Galaxy Bias]]. $\delta_g = b\delta$. On large enough scales, the relation of galaxy and DM is simple, and b is almost Constant.

# Measurements
Need [[Spectrosopy]] Surveys or [[photometry]] surveys (imaging surveys).
- List of Imaging surveys: POSS/APM, DES, SDSS,... (Photometric redshift (lower accuracy))
- List of spectroscopy surveys: SDSS...(Spectroscopic redshift)

These measurements show signs of the [[Baryon Acoustic Oscillation]] and also succesfully rules out HotDM models and sCDM (without Dark Energy), and is a success for [[LCDM model]].
