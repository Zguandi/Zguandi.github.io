The dominant equations in the [[Large Scale Structure]] formation are:
1. Continuity Equation (from 0st moment of [[DM perturbation]]) $\frac{\partial\delta}{\partial t}+\frac{1}{a}\nabla \cdot \vec{v}=0$
2. Euler Equation (from 1st moment of DM perturbation) $\frac{\partial \vec{v}}{\partial t}+\frac{\dot{a}}{a}\vec{v}=-\frac{\nabla \Phi}{a}$
3. Poisson Equation (from [[Einstein'e Equation perturbation]]) $\nabla^2 \Phi=4\pi G \bar{\rho}a^2 \delta$.

The Large Scale velocities gives the prediction for [[Peculiar Velocity]] at the linear regime.
