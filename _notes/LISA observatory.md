Here is the notes taken from lecturer [Philippe Jetzer](https://www.physik.uzh.ch/en/groups/jetzer/People/Philippe-Jetzer.html) from universitat Zurich.

[[Gravitational waves]] is a new window to explore the universe. The existing gw detectors [LIGO](https://www.ligo.caltech.edu/)-Virgo-Karga, the design is a giant **Michelson-Interferometer**.

With the detected precision, it is equivalent of measureing 1/100 of the proton diameter $r_p$, over a length $l=4km$.

Buiding more detectors means we can determine the exact celestrial coordinate where the signal is originating from. The frequency is in the order of $100 Hz$.

Other ways of doing GW measurement includes **Pulsar Timing Array** and **Atomic Interferometers**

The scientific goals of **LISA** is:
1. Binary White Dwarfs, binary Neutron Stars.
2. **Extreme Mass-ratio In-spirals** *(EMRI)*.
3. Coaescence of **Supermassive Blackholes**.
4. [[Primordial gravitational waves]].

The LISA project consists of three satellites arranging in triangular shapes in the same orbit of the earth around the sun.

The main concern of a space-based GW detector is that the test masses should be on the geodesics. The external casing is decoupled from the mass and absorbs all the external gas collisions.
