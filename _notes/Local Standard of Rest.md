For [[Stellar dynamics]], it is sometimes usefull to depict all velocities in the LSR reference frame. See [LSR Wiki Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_standard_of_rest#:~:text=In%20astronomy%2C%20the%20local%20standard,Milky%20Way%20as%20the%20Sun.)


![Local_standard_of_rest](/assets/pictures/Local_standard_of_rest.png)
In the new coordinate system, we measure velocity in the co-rotating  frame.

> [!QUESTION] The rotation of Local Standard of Rest
> The LSR is defined **Only to work in the neighboourhood** of the solar system. The coordinate centre is defined by a local population of stars that is rotating around the galactic centre, and the radial coordinate $\Pi$ is fixed to point towards the galactic centre.

In this frame, we can measure speed projected onto the R, theta, Z directions (actually orthorgonal basis in LSR).

$$
u(radial),v(rotation),w(height)
$$
