The tSZ is the effect of [[Cosmic Microwave Background]] photons being **Inverse Compton Scattered** by hot electrons within galaxies, and shows an elevation of energy from the blackbody spectrum. The Amount of Deviation is defined by the [[Thermal SZ|Compton Y]] parameter.

The CMB has a temperature fluatuation caused by inverse compton scattering of hot gas electrons with the CMb photons. The temperature shift is discribed in the Line-of-sight pressure integral:
$$
\delta T_{SZ}=-\frac{\sigma_T T_{CMB} F(x)}{m_e c^2}\int P_e dr
$$
where $F(x)=4-x\coth(x/2)$ and $x=h\nu/k_BT_{CMB}$.

The SZ flux:
$$
\delta \bar{T}_{SZ}\delta \Omega =\int_{\delta\Omega}\delta T_{SZ}(\nu,\hat{n})d^2 \hat{n}
$$
By separation of variables:
$$
\frac{\delta T}{T_{CMB}}=f(\nu) y(\theta)
$$
The y parameter is called the [[Thermal SZ|Compton Y]], and has an angular dependence:
$$
y(\hat{n})=\frac{\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}\int_{LOS} P_e dl
$$
The tSZ is a probe of thermal energy of the matter distribution in the universe.
