Protoplanetary Disk =  Circumstellar Disk

Place where planet formation happens. ususally around [[Young stars]].

# Composition
99% gas, 1% dust(sillicates and other stuff)

# Disk radius
Observed disks of exoplanets are distributed from ~0.01AU to 10AU

A lot is coming from observational bias.
