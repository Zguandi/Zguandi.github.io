# Dynamic Equillibrium
A spherical distribution of gas, assuming the gas reaches stability, the Equations involve:
$$
-\nabla P = \rho \frac{d \vec{v}}{dt}\tag{1}-\rho \vec{g}
$$
And the Euler Equation:
$$
\frac{d \vec{v}}{dt}=\frac{\partial \vec{v}}{\partial t}+(\vec{v} \cdot\nabla)\vec{v}\tag{2}
$$

This combines into:
$$
\frac{\partial \vec{v}}{\partial t}+(\vec{v} \cdot\nabla)\vec{v}+\frac{\nabla P}{\rho}-\vec{g}=0
$$
With the [[Virialisation|Virial Theorem]], we know the Kinetic energy $K$ and Potential $W$, has $K=-W/2=3/2 m k_B T$ when the system reaches equillibrium.
