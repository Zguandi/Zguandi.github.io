The Age of the Universe can be computed floowing the [[Friedmann Equations]]:
$$
\begin{align}
T &= \int_0^T dt=\int_0^{a_0} \frac{1}{a}\frac{a}{\dot{a}}da\\
&=\int_0^{a_0}\frac{da}{aH_0}[\Omega_{\Lambda,0}+\Omega_{m,0}(a_0/a)^3+\Omega_{r,0}(a_0/a)^4+\Omega_{k,0}(a_0/a)^2]^{-1/2}
\end{align}
$$
After Integration, the result is in the same scale of the $H_0^{-1}$, and yields a value of $T = 13.7 Gyr$.
